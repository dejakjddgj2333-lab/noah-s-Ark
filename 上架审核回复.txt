【1】App 用途与目标用户 (Purpose & Target Audience)

明策（Noah's Ark / Mingce）是一款加密货币市场行情数据终端与社交工具。
面向加密货币投资者与交易者，解决"市场信息分散、链上动向难追踪、
风险预警不及时"的问题，提供多维市场指数（恐慌贪婪、多空爆仓、
资金费率、巨鲸雷达）、实时行情与自定义价格预警、7×24 资讯、
以及内置的好友聊天与音视频通话，帮助用户在一个终端内掌握市场全貌。

明策 (Mingce) is a crypto market data terminal and social tool for crypto
investors and traders. It solves the problems of scattered market information,
hard-to-track on-chain movements and delayed risk alerts, by providing
multi-dimensional indices (Fear & Greed, liquidations, funding rates, whale
radar), live quotes with custom price alerts, 24/7 news, and built-in chat
with voice/video calls — everything in one terminal.

The App is a data-display and social tool ONLY. It does NOT provide fiat
deposits, crypto trading/matching, custody, clearing, investment advisory,
or asset management. All market data comes from public third-party exchange
APIs and is for reference only.

【3】外部服务 / 数据源 (External Services)

本 App 仅展示公开行情数据，不接入任何支付处理。所用外部服务：
- Binance / Bybit / OKX / CoinGecko / CoinGlass — 公开行情、资金费率、爆仓数据
- Alternative.me — 恐慌贪婪指数
- TronGrid / Etherscan / BSCScan / Arbiscan — 链上转账与地址查询
- APITube — 资讯采集
- DeepSeek — 资讯摘要与宏观日历中文翻译（AI 生成，非核心功能）
- Apple APNs — 推送通知

No payment processors are used. The App does not sell digital goods or
subscriptions; there is no In-App Purchase.

本 App 功能在所有地区一致，无地区差异。
行情与资讯为全球通用内容；支持 8 种界面语言（中/英/日/韩/西/葡/印地语）。
The App functions consistently across all regions, with no regional

【5】资质 / 受监管行业 (Credentials)

The App is a data display and social tool. It does not offer fiat deposits,

【6】账号删除 (Account Deletion)

已支持账号注销：我的 → 安全设置 → 注销账号（输入登录密码确认后永久停用）。
同时支持用户内容举报与拉黑：聊天长按消息举报，右上角菜单拉黑用户，
后台管理系统可处理举报（删消息/封号）。
