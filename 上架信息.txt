—— 中文版 ——

明策是一款专业级加密货币行情终端，为交易者提供多维度市场数据、
链上巨鲸动向与实时风险预警，帮你在瞬息万变的市场中抢占先机。

◆ 多维市场指数
• 综合行情总览：恐慌贪婪指数、多空对比、全网热度一目了然
• 多空爆仓：实时监控 Binance/Bybit 强平数据，洞察杠杆风险
• 资金费率：主流交易所永续合约费率对比与历史走势
• 巨鲸雷达：追踪链上大额转账与持仓异动，紧跟聪明钱

◆ 实时行情与预警
• 全市场行情列表，涨跌幅、成交额实时刷新
• 自定义价格预警：涨破/跌破目标价即时推送
• 专业 K 线与盘口深度，支撑位压力位清晰可见

◆ 资讯与社区
• 7×24 快讯、深度资讯与官方公告，多语言阅读
• 内置聊天：好友私聊、群组讨论，文字/图片/语音/视频
• 音视频通话，与伙伴实时沟通行情

◆ 安全与账户
• 登录密码、资金密码、谷歌验证 (2FA) 多重保护
• 登录设备管理，异常设备一键下线
• 防钓鱼码，识别官方通知真伪

◆ 智能推送
行情预警、新消息、好友请求、提现到账——在线即时提醒，
离线 APNs 推送，重要动态绝不错过。

—— English ——

Mingce is a professional crypto market terminal for traders, delivering
multi-dimensional market data, on-chain whale tracking and real-time
risk alerts to keep you ahead of the market.

◆ Multi-Dimensional Indices
• Market overview: Fear & Greed index, long/short ratio, global heat
• Liquidations: real-time Binance/Bybit liquidation monitoring
• Funding rates: perpetual funding comparison & history
• Whale radar: track large on-chain transfers & position shifts

◆ Real-Time Quotes & Alerts
• Full market list with live price & volume updates
• Custom price alerts: notified instantly on breakout/breakdown
• Pro candlesticks & order book depth

◆ News & Community
• 24/7 flash news, articles & official announcements, multilingual

• Voice & video calls to discuss the market live

◆ Security & Account
• Login password, fund password & Google Authenticator (2FA)
• Device management: sign out any device remotely
• Anti-phishing code to verify official notifications

◆ Smart Notifications
Price alerts, new messages, friend requests & withdrawal arrivals —
instant in-app alerts online, APNs push when offline. Never miss a move.
